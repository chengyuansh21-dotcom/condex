# Style Presets

Use these presets as starting points. Always adapt them to the uploaded image and user's intent.

## default-presentation

Purpose: general report/PPT quality improvement.

Enhance realistic materials, cleaner daylight, soft shadows, moderate planting, natural people scale, readable urban context, clear sky, and balanced color grading. Keep the scene professional and restrained.

## premium-commercial

Purpose: high-end commercial street, retail facade, shopping block,招商展示.

Enhance premium facade materials, warm storefront lighting, refined paving, active but uncrowded pedestrians, subtle outdoor seating, signage zones without inventing readable brand text, clean window reflections, and a polished commercial atmosphere. Avoid luxury exaggeration or changing the facade design.

## urban-renewal

Purpose: city renewal, old street improvement, community-facing public realm.

Enhance realistic street life, repaired pavement, human-scale lighting, street furniture, restrained greenery, local Chinese urban context, improved facade cleanliness, and a believable everyday atmosphere. Preserve existing street width, building age, and neighborhood character.

## night-commercial

Purpose: night scene conversion for commercial streets and public buildings.

Preserve original geometry and camera position. Add warm interior glow, storefront lighting, controlled facade highlights, wet or subtly reflective paving only when appropriate, evening sky, and balanced contrast. Avoid over-cinematic neon, excessive bloom, and illegible signage.

## residential-entrance

Purpose: residential gate, lobby entrance, community arrival view.

Enhance warm welcoming light, stone/metal/wood texture, entry canopy detail, planting layers, calm pedestrian scale, clean paving, and a refined residential tone. Avoid turning the project into a hotel or mall.

## interior-retail

Purpose: interior commercial spaces, showrooms, lobbies, restaurants, public interiors.

Enhance material tactility, ceiling and wall lighting, furniture quality, people scale, product/display clarity, depth of field only where subtle, and clean reflections. Preserve circulation, partitions, storefront openings, ceiling structure, and major furniture layout.

## landscape-node

Purpose: landscape node, park corner, courtyard, plaza, waterfront, community open space.

Enhance planting hierarchy, ground texture, sunlight/shadow, benches, subtle activity, water or paving detail if already present, and seasonal atmosphere. Avoid dense random vegetation that hides the design.

## aerial-masterplan

Purpose: bird's-eye or aerial masterplan atmosphere.

Enhance roof/ground clarity, landscape patches, street hierarchy, soft atmospheric depth, cars/people only at scale-appropriate size, and presentation-level sharpness. Preserve block layout, roads, building footprints, and open-space structure.

## material-only

Purpose: improve surfaces without changing life/props.

Enhance facade texture, glass reflections, stone/metal/wood/concrete realism, shadow contact, edge clarity, and subtle weathering. Do not add people, trees, signs, furniture, or new objects unless asked.

## people-landscape-only

Purpose: enrich scale and activity while preserving architecture.

Add or refine human-scale pedestrians, planting, street furniture, and landscape accents. Keep density moderate, avoid blocking the main building, and maintain realistic scale and local context.
